2) What the app is for
Aftercoda is a personal food-and-pulse log for people who already suspect a meal is tied to how they feel later — a headache, a gut flare, something on the skin — and want a simple way to see whether that meal sat inside the same lookback window more than once.

The home screen is today’s 24-hour ring. You stamp Head, Gut, or Skin at an hour; that region lights its own coda (lookback, 0–6 hours). You can only bind a meal that already sits in that sector, or type one in. A pairing shows up on the report after two matches. Score is matched over total pulses, not calories.

It is a counted log, not a diagnosis and not a medical device. We say that in the app.

Target audience: adults who already keep some kind of food / symptom notes and find a regular journal messy. We are not aiming at clinics or at people looking for a calorie counter.

3) How to get around
No login, no demo account, no sample files. Just open the app.

- If the briefing comes up, tap Next through the four pages and Open the drum, or tap Skip.
- On Timeline, pick Head, Gut, or Skin. Scrub the ring or tap Now, then Stamp Head / Stamp Gut / Stamp Skin.
- If you see “The drum is blank,” tap Stamp epicenter.
- Tap Bind, type something like “oats, tea” (comma or a new line splits stations), then Sew typed stations. You can also bind a station that is already listed in the coda.
- Pairings opens “Meals inside each coda.” A pairing needs two matches; otherwise it stays empty.
- Coda explains the region-owned lookback.
- Lookbacks lets you retune Head / Gut / Skin (0–6 hours), export the local pairing text, open Contact, replay the briefing, or reset everything.

Contact is in Lookbacks and goes to https://aftercoda.pro/contact-us.

4) Services behind the app
The log itself lives on the device (day files on the phone). We do not use a sign-in service, payment processor, or AI service. The only site we point people to is our own support page, https://aftercoda.pro/contact-us.

5) Regions
Same app everywhere. Hours and scores follow the device locale. Factory lookbacks are Head 6h, Gut 3h, Skin 6h. Contact URL is the same worldwide.

Not applicable. Personal log only. No licensed medical content, no clinic affiliation, no protected third-party material.
